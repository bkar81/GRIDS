Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Short version

GRIDS is designed so that its application configuration is stored locally in your browser. GRIDS does not require a GRIDS account or a GRIDS-operated application server for its normal configuration and form-submission workflow. Google services are contacted when a feature that requires Google is used, such as Google authentication, Sheet access, or optional Drive synchronization.

What GRIDS stores locally

GRIDS stores its configuration in browser storage. This can include the Google Form link, Google Sheet link, Form title, configured Form fields, Google Form entry IDs, default values, and report configuration.

The app also keeps local state needed for its optional synchronization workflow. Resetting GRIDS removes the locally stored GRIDS configuration on that device.

Google Form submissions

When you submit an entry from Home, the values you enter are sent to the Google Form configured by you. GRIDS does not operate the destination Google Form and does not control how Google stores or processes submitted Form responses.

Google Sheet access

The Report feature can read data from the Google Sheet you configure. Access requires Google authentication and the Google Sheets read-only permission used by GRIDS. GRIDS uses the Sheet data to generate the configured report in your browser.

Entering a Google Sheet link by itself does not give GRIDS access to the Sheet; access is requested when a Google-connected feature requires it.

Google Drive sync

Drive synchronization is optional. When you explicitly connect Google and use GRIDS synchronization feature, GRIDS can read and write its own configuration file, grids-settings_<application name>.json, in your Google Drive using Google's drive.file permission.

Your Google authentication and Drive data remain associated with your Google account. GRIDS does not use a separate GRIDS server to receive or store your Drive data.

Google authentication

Google authentication is used for features that require Google access, such as reading the linked Sheet or synchronizing GRIDS settings with Drive. Authentication credentials and tokens are not included in GRIDS exported JSON configuration.

JSON export and import

GRIDS lets you export its configuration to a JSON file for backup or transfer. Google authentication credentials and tokens are not included in the exported settings.

Cookies, analytics and tracking

GRIDS application code does not include an application analytics or advertising system. The application does not require cookies for its local configuration workflow. Third-party Google services used through authentication and APIs may have their own technologies, policies, and data practices.

Offline and PWA behavior

GRIDS can be installed as a Progressive Web App. Its service worker provides the application shell for offline use. Google-connected features naturally require the relevant Google service and network access.

Your control

You control the Google Form, Google Sheet, Google account, and Drive configuration that you connect to GRIDS. You can export your GRIDS configuration, import a backup, manage Google access through your Google account, or use Settings → Sync → Reset app data to clear the current Application's locally stored configuration on this device.

Sharing and self-hosting

A separate hosted or forked copy of GRIDS can have its own Google OAuth configuration and its own Form, Sheet, and Drive settings. Operators of separate deployments are responsible for configuring their own Google services and for complying with applicable privacy and legal requirements.

Applications and backups

GRIDS can hold several Applications, each with its own settings stored in this browser. It also keeps one last-known-working copy of each Application on the device. Backup files you export (one Application or all Applications) are saved where you choose and are yours to keep safe. Google authentication tokens are never included.

Questions

Questions about GRIDS privacy behavior can be raised through the project's published contact or repository information.
